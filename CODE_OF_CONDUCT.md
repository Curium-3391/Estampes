# Estampes community code of conduct

Estampes welcomes people who want to investigate, build, critique, document, and use visual tools with AI. The same standards apply in Issues, Discussions, pull requests, code review, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Challenge technical claims and code, not people's identities or motives.
- Assume good intent at first, and accept corrections when new evidence appears.
- Explain what you tested, what you observed, and what remains uncertain.
- Respect the time of volunteer maintainers and other contributors.
- Protect privacy: never publish credentials, private images, sensitive logs, or another person's personal information.
- Make room for different experience levels and language backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hateful or discriminatory language, intimidation, sexualized abuse, repeated personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to distribute malware, leaked credentials, or exploit instructions aimed at harming other people.

## Moderation and reporting

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove violating contributions, and may restrict participation when necessary. Responses are proportionate to severity and context, including warnings or removal from community spaces.

To report a conduct problem privately, contact the maintainer using the contact address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), with the subject "Estampes community conduct report". Include relevant links and a short description; do not share sensitive details publicly.

For technical security vulnerabilities, use the separate private reporting process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This is a volunteer-run project. Moderation and support are provided on a best-effort basis.
